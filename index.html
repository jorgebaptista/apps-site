Personal mail agent

A private tool that lets Jorge Baptista's AI assistants read and organise his own Gmail accounts.

What it is

Personal mail agent is the Google sign-in for a small server that runs on Jorge's own computer. It connects his Gmail accounts to the AI assistants he uses, so he can ask them to search his mail, sort it into labels, archive or delete messages, manage filters, and prepare or send replies he has approved.

Who can use it

Only Jorge Baptista, with his own Google accounts. It is not offered to anyone else, and there is nothing to sign up for.
